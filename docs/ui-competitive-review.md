**Goal:** identify the top-performing UI for this product category, unequivocally.
**Method:** full-page teardowns fetched live: CertifiedPayrollPro (modern standalone SaaS), PrevailComply (design-forward indie, captured 2026-08-06), eBacon (platform/sales-led), Payroll4Construction interactive WH-347 page (content/SEO leader), pdfFiller's WH-347 page (the SERP winner for form-seeker queries). ## The unequivocal verdict

**The top-performing UI in this category is the "document-as-hero, free-first" architecture: pdfFiller's zero-friction tool pattern fused with CertifiedPayrollPro's conversion scaffolding.** Concretely, the formula the evidence supports:

1. **The form itself is the hero visual.** Buyers recognize the WH-347 document, not abstract dashboards. Both #1 and #2 lead with a rendered form. Text-only heroes lose this audience.
2. **One dominant free CTA, friction-killers enumerated beneath it** ("free · no signup · no credit card"). The free action must be reachable in one click and completable without an account.
3. **Quantified time + fear**: "minutes not hours" plus a compliance-penalty stat. This buyer is motivated by Friday-evening time and DOL fear in equal parts.
4. **State chips near the hero** (Federal · California · Texas…): instant relevance + internal-link equity to the SEO pages.
5. **Trust hardware**: named testimonials with specific numbers, security claims, ratings. (We cannot fake these — they get added as real ones arrive.)
6. **Transparent pricing with a comparison/savings device** — the whole category's legacy tier hides pricing; publishing it is itself a conversion weapon (CPP explicitly markets "we publish ours because we're proud of it").
7. **A guide persona lowers fear** (CPP's "Lydia"). Our structural analogue is stronger: the dual-model verify screen — *show it* on the landing page (green confirmed rows, yellow review rows) as proof that nothing is silently guessed.
8. **Long-form SEO content and FAQ live below the tool**, never above it.
